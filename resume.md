## Portfolio Projects

### Tuition & AR Aging Dashboard (Excel) — Summer 2026
*Portfolio project · [GitHub repo](https://github.com/Oirat-nomad/account-receivable-aging-dashboard)*

Built an Excel dashboard modeling **current-term tuition receivables** for a bursar / student accounts office, using **280 synthetic billing records** across five academic programs.

- Designed aging logic around **DueToDate_Amount**, **OldestPastDueDate**, and **BalanceDue** so outstanding balances reflect payment-plan schedules—not full-term charges when installments are active
- Built a four-sheet workbook: KPI dashboard, AR ledger with formulas, pivot analysis (aging bucket × program), and data dictionary
- Delivered executive-ready KPIs as of 07/13/2026: **$521,833 outstanding AR**, **183 open accounts**, **57.1% collection rate**
- Identified that **81% of outstanding AR sits in the 61–90 day bucket**, enabling prioritized collections before accounts reach 90+ days and require escalated follow-up
- Applied `SUMIFS`, `COUNTIFS`, nested `IF`, pivot tables, and pie charts to summarize AR aging, payment plan status (On Plan / Not on Plan / Defaulted), and outstanding balances by program

**Tools:** Excel · Pivot Tables · AR Aging · Higher Ed Finance
